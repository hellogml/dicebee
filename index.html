How the roll works

  1. Choose your dice. Pick the number of dice and the face count that fits your game — classic d6, or the tabletop favorites.
  2. Random, not guesswork. Each die uses your browser's secure random generator, so every face is just as likely as the next and no pattern repeats.
  3. Watch and read. The dice tumble, then land on clear values you can read at a glance.
  4. Add it all up. Total, average, min and max are computed for every round automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which dice can I roll?

Any combination of D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20 and D100, with 1 to 20 dice in a single roll.

Are the dice rolls fair and random?

Yes. Every result comes from your browser’s cryptographically secure random number generator, so each face has an equal chance and nothing is predetermined.

Can I see the history of my rolls?

Yes. Each roll, its individual dice and the running total are added to the roll history so you can review the whole session.

Do I need an account to roll dice?

No. The dice roller is free, runs entirely in your browser and needs no account, download or sign-up.
